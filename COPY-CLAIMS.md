# PayMyDine copy and claims policy

## Numbers currently used on the website

The website uses product counts that are supported by the current product map:

- 9 product areas
- 6 role workspaces
- 5 operating-flow steps
- 3 bill-split methods
- 9 management and profitability metrics
- 7 reservation and seating capabilities
- 7 ordering, kitchen and menu controls
- 8 guest-ordering and payment moments
- 6 AI-assisted actions
- 4 integration types
- 6 multi-location and central controls
- 6 implementation stages

These are product-scope counts, not customer performance claims.

## Performance claims

Do not publish claims such as 30% faster, 20% more revenue or 40% fewer errors until PayMyDine has:

1. a documented customer baseline,
2. a defined measurement method,
3. a comparison period,
4. a sample size,
5. written approval to publish the result.

## Metrics to baseline during implementation

Choose the metrics that match the workflow being implemented. Examples include:

- reservation wait time
- seating time
- table turnover
- order-entry time
- kitchen preparation time
- ready-to-serve handoff time
- payment completion time
- average check
- guest count or covers
- repeat-visit rate
- campaign response
- sold-out frequency
- food cost
- revenue or profitability by item or location

Website copy should explain what can be tracked or improved without inventing a guaranteed percentage.
